OVERVIEW

Real-Time Whole-Body Teleoperation

A compact overview of the system and its main real-world demonstration.

OVERVIEW DEMO System Overview Demonstration assets/videos/overview-demo.mp4

Overview demonstration video. This is separate from the landing-page background video.

Real-time humanoid teleoperation requires a controller to reproduce human intent while respecting the robot's morphology, balance, contacts, and actuator constraints. Directly tracking human motion can produce infeasible lower-body references and unstable behavior, especially during deep squats, crouching, single-leg support, and fast whole-body motions. We introduce a framework that explicitly maps human motion into a robot-feasible motion representation before control. The resulting policy executes expressive whole-body motion online while maintaining physical feasibility and stability. The framework is designed for real-time teleoperation and supports both simulation and physical humanoid deployment.
